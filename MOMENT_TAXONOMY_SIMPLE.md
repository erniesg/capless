# Moment Taxonomy - Simplified
**13 clear categories with NO overlap + 1 catch-all**

---

## 🎯 PHILOSOPHY

**Structure + Diversity + Surprises:**
- Extract EVERYTHING viral-worthy (cast wide net)
- Categorize when clear (12 specific categories)
- Use WILDCARD when unclear (20-30% of moments)
- Categories are DESCRIPTIVE not PRESCRIPTIVE

**Key Principle:**
> "If it's TikTok-worthy, extract it. Categories come second."

---

## 🎬 TWO-STAGE EXTRACTION STRATEGY

### Stage 1: Cast Wide Net (UNRESTRICTED)
**Goal:** Find ANY TikTok-worthy viral moment

**Criteria:**
- Quotable (15-40 words)
- Makes people react ("Wait what?", "OMG", "So true", "Finally!")
- Shareable to friends/family
- Explains itself without much context
- ANY emotion (anger, joy, confusion, hope, outrage, relief)

**Don't self-censor!** If it's shareable → extract it.

**Expected:** 20-50 moments per session

---

### Stage 2: Soft Categorization (OPTIONAL TAGGING)
**Goal:** Organize moments when categorization is clear

**Process:**
1. For each extracted moment, TRY to tag with a category
2. If it CLEARLY fits → tag it
3. If uncertain OR hybrid → tag as "wildcard"
4. Include confidence level: high/medium/low

**Result:** Structured data without losing surprises

---

## 🔄 LEARNING LOOP

**Wildcards reveal new patterns:**
```
Monthly review → Cluster wildcards by similarity → Find patterns

Examples discovered:
- 15 wildcards = "Awkward Silence" moments
- 10 wildcards = "Wholesome Cross-Party Friendship"
- 8 wildcards = "Technical Glitch Comedy"

If pattern count > 10 → Promote to official category
```

**Categories EVOLVE organically from wildcards** 🌱

## 📊 THE 13 CATEGORIES

### 1. 🙈 DON'T ASK ME
**What it is:** Evasion, "We don't track that", refusing to disclose

**Clear identifier:**
- Contains phrases: "don't track", "can't disclose", "not in public interest"
- Minister claims ignorance of data they should have
- Deflecting with "confidentiality" excuses

**Examples:**
- HDB: "We don't collect info on self-employed owners"
- Edwin Tong: "We don't know the cost"
- "Not in public interest to disclose reasons"

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "Walao eh! You're the government and you DON'T KNOW?!"
- **Auntie:** "How to plan ahead if they don't even track! So stress!"
- **Gen Z:** "Bro said 'we don't track' like data doesn't exist 💀"
- **Attenborough:** "*whispers* The bureaucrat, when cornered, retreats into ignorance..."

**NOT this category:**
- If they answer but deflect to citizens → Push Ball (#2)
- If they answer but it's theatre → Wayang King (#4)

---

### 2. 😤 PUSH BALL
**What it is:** Deflecting responsibility TO CITIZENS (not to other MPs)

**Clear identifier:**
- Solutions require citizens to adapt, not government to act
- "Just ventilate", "be flexible", "make adjustments"
- Problem displacement from government to individuals

**Examples:**
- "Fix formaldehyde? Just ventilate"
- "Insurance expensive? Just be flexible"
- "Hawker uncle must use fewer ingredients"

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "This one is CLASSIC push ball tactic! Minister kick problem to homeowner!"
- **Auntie:** "They say 'flexible' but actually means we die our own business right?"
- **Gen Z:** "It's giving 'not my problem' energy fr fr"
- **Attenborough:** "Notice how the burden transfers from regulator to citizen..."

**NOT this category:**
- If they deflect to other MPs → Ownself Check Ownself (#3)
- If they refuse to answer → Don't Ask Me (#1)

---

### 3. 🪞 OWNSELF CHECK OWNSELF
**What it is:** Self-regulation, conflicts of interest, circular accountability

**Clear identifier:**
- Government investigating itself
- Same entity is judge and defendant
- "We reviewed ourselves and found no issues"
- Lack of independent oversight

**Examples:**
- PUB investigating PUB misconduct
- Minister reviewing Minister's department
- Internal audit clearing internal problems
- "We've conducted our own review..."

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "Liddat also can ah? Ownself check ownself, confirm will pass one lah!"
- **Auntie:** "Aiyoh! Like asking the fox to guard the chicken coop! How like that?"
- **Gen Z:** "The conflict of interest is conflicting 💀 make it make sense"
- **Attenborough:** "A fascinating circular pattern emerges when oversight oversights itself..."

**NOT this category:**
- If it's just evasion without self-regulation → Don't Ask Me (#1)
- If it's performative → Wayang King (#4)

---

### 4. 🎭 WAYANG KING/QUEEN
**What it is:** Political theatre, performative concern, tone-deaf responses

**Clear identifier:**
- Praise immediately after criticism/scandal
- Dismissive reactions ("you missed the reports")
- Actions don't match words
- Political kabuki

**Examples:**
- Teo Chee Hean: "You missed the reports" (after audit failures)
- "八字都还没一撇" - too early to discuss (but problem is urgent)
- Thanking Minister during their scandal discussion

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "Wah this one wayang power level 9000! NATO - No Action, Talk Only!"
- **Auntie:** "My friend's daughter say this kind is 'gaslighting' right? Cannot be like that!"
- **Gen Z:** "The AUDACITY. Tell me you're out of touch without telling me you're out of touch 💀"
- **Attenborough:** "*whispers* Political theater in its purest form..."

**NOT this category:**
- If it's absurd logic → Say What Now? (#5)
- If it's just evasion → Don't Ask Me (#1)

---

### 5. 🤯 SAY WHAT NOW?
**What it is:** ABSURD LOGIC - contradictions, illogical reasoning, mental gymnastics

**Clear identifier:**
- The math ain't mathing
- Contradictory statements in same breath
- "Wait, run that by me again?"
- Oxymorons and paradoxes
- Explanation more confusing than problem

**Examples:**
- "Excessive competition gone wrong" (competed TOO HARD to help you)
- "17 months jail for billions in money laundering"
- "Carbon tax won't raise net revenue" (then why?)
- "Mandatory voluntary participation"

**NOT this category:**
- If it's evasion → Don't Ask Me (#1)
- If it's just bureaucratic jargon → belongs here if ALSO illogical

---

### 6. 💚 SOLID MOVE
**What it is:** GOOD GOVERNANCE - bold commitments, actual accountability, transparency

**Clear identifier:**
- Clear action + clear timeline
- Admission of mistakes + plan to fix
- Transparent numbers/data
- Direct, honest answers
- Pragmatic solutions

**Examples:**
- "Body-worn cameras by March 2025" (after scandal)
- "Formaldehyde paints banned from 2026"
- Minister admitting: "We got this wrong, here's the fix"
- Cross-party collaboration

**NOT this category:**
- If it's just direct talk without action → Straight Talk (#12)
- If it's performative → Wayang King (#4)

---

### 7. 🌍 ATTENBOROUGH-WORTHY
**What it is:** EPIC OBSERVATIONS with documentary-style narration (can be sincere OR sarcastic)

**Clear identifier:**
- Nature documentary vibes
- Sweeping societal observations
- Profound metaphors
- Can be ironic/sarcastic about political rituals
- "Here, in the parliamentary chamber, we observe..."

**Examples (Sincere):**
- Josephine Teo: "Illicit money flows like toxic waste in the ocean..."
- Louis Ng: "Food does not come naturally to Singapore..."

**Examples (Sarcastic/Ironic):**
- "Notice how the Minister retreats into complexity when cornered... a classic defensive adaptation..."
- "The bureaucrat, sensing danger, deploys the 'we don't track that' defense mechanism..."

**NOT this category:**
- If it's just data → Receipts Please (#8)
- If it's just good policy → Solid Move (#6)

---

### 8. 👀 RECEIPTS PLEASE
**What it is:** SHOCKING DATA/STATISTICS that expose reality

**Clear identifier:**
- Hard numbers that tell a story
- Data contradicts official narrative
- Statistics that make you go "WHAT?!"
- Fine print that reveals truth

**Examples:**
- "750K tonnes food waste, 1.6M imported = nearly half thrown away"
- "Only 19 formaldehyde complaints since 2020" (really??)
- "9,600 money mule investigations in 2023"

**NOT this category:**
- If there's no specific data → not this
- If it's observational → Attenborough-Worthy (#7)

---

### 9. 😱 KIASU AUNTIE APPROVED
**What it is:** LEGITIMATE WORRIES about family/money/safety/future

**Clear identifier:**
- Affects family finances directly
- Housing/education/healthcare concerns
- "What about my children??" moments
- Forward-planning anxieties
- Must be LEGITIMATE concern (not absurd)

**Examples:**
- Insurance premiums rising + can't regulate
- Housing accessibility waivers
- Hawker value meals = less protein for kids
- Education costs spiraling

**NOT this category:**
- If it's absurd logic → Say What Now? (#5)
- If it's evasion → Don't Ask Me (#1)

---

### 10. 🎤 MIC DROP
**What it is:** DEVASTATING COMEBACKS or closing statements

**Clear identifier:**
- Perfect rebuttal
- Catches opponent in contradiction
- "Enough said" energy
- Moment that ends the debate
- Quotable finisher

**Examples:**
- MP catches Minister contradicting their past statement
- Pointed question that exposes hypocrisy
- Closing statement that summarizes the absurdity perfectly

**NOT this category:**
- If it's just heated → Spicy Exchange (#11)
- If it's about data → Receipts Please (#8)

---

### 11. 🔥 SPICY EXCHANGE
**What it is:** HEATED DEBATE - high tension, raised voices, conflict

**Clear identifier:**
- Temperature is HIGH
- Back-and-forth escalation
- Pointed accusations
- Defensive replies
- Drama!

**Examples:**
- MPs interrupting each other
- Voice levels rising
- "That's not what I said!" energy
- Tense confrontations

**NOT this category:**
- If it ends with mic drop → Mic Drop (#10)
- If it's theatrical but not genuinely heated → Wayang King (#4)

---

### 12. ✅ STRAIGHT TALK
**What it is:** DIRECT, HONEST ANSWERS without corporate speak

**Clear identifier:**
- "Yes" or "No" to yes/no questions
- Plain language, no jargon
- Candid admission
- No beating around the bush
- Refreshingly clear

**Examples:**
- MP asks: "Will you commit to this?" Minister: "Yes, by Q2 2025"
- Complex issue explained in simple terms
- No deflection, just facts

**NOT this category:**
- If there's action commitment → Solid Move (#6)
- If it's evasion → Don't Ask Me (#1)

---

### 13. 🎲 WILDCARD
**What it is:** VIRAL-WORTHY but doesn't fit any category above

**Clear identifier:**
- You watched it and thought "THIS IS VIRAL!"
- But none of the 12 categories feel right
- Unique, one-off moments
- Meme material
- Unexpected humor
- Wholesome/heartwarming that isn't "Solid Move"
- Bizarre but not illogical enough for "Say What Now?"

**Examples:**
- Unexpected humor/jokes
- Awkward silences that go viral
- Wholesome cross-party friendships
- Technical glitches that became moments
- Anything genuinely TikTok-worthy that defies categorization

**This is the catch-all - when in doubt, use this!**
