## 📊 THE 8 VOTING CATEGORIES

### 1. 🙈 "DON'T ASK ME" Moment
**What it is:**
- "We don't track that"
- "Not in public interest to disclose"
- Bureaucratic evasion disguised as policy
- Accountability dodging

**Examples:**
- HDB: "We don't collect info on self-employed owners"
- Edwin Tong: "We don't know the cost, it's part of overall contract"
- Minister: "Not in public interest to disclose reasons"

**Why it works:**
- Immediately understandable
- Captures the frustration
- Meme-able format

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "Walao eh! You're the government and you DON'T KNOW?!"
- **Auntie:** "How to plan ahead if they don't even track! So stress!"
- **Gen Z:** "Bro said 'we don't track' like data doesn't exist 💀"
- **Attenborough:** "*whispers* The bureaucrat, when cornered, retreats into ignorance..."

---

### 2. 😤 "PUSH BALL" Moment
**What it is:**
- Deflecting responsibility to citizens
- "Just ventilate" solutions
- Problem displacement to individuals
- Passing the buck

**Examples:**
- "Fix formaldehyde? Just ventilate"
- "Insurance expensive? Not expected to make loss, just be flexible"
- "Can't afford? Uncle must use fewer ingredients"

**Why it works:**
- Classic Singlish term everyone knows
- Instantly conveys the frustration
- Familiar coping mechanism

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "This one is CLASSIC push ball tactic! Minister kick problem to homeowner!"
- **Auntie:** "They say 'flexible' but actually means we die our own business right?"
- **Gen Z:** "It's giving 'not my problem' energy fr fr"
- **Attenborough:** "Notice how burden transfers from regulator to citizen..."

---

### 3. 🎭 "WAYANG KING/QUEEN" Moment
**What it is:**
- Political theatre
- Tone-deaf responses after scandals
- Dismissive reactions (Teo Chee Hean: "you missed the reports")
- Performative concern without action

**Examples:**
- Senior Minister dismisses Jamus after audit failures
- "八字都还没一撇" - too early to talk about concerns
- Praise for someone immediately after they got grilled

**Why it works:**
- "Wayang" is peak Singaporean
- Everyone knows political theatre
- Fun to call out

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "Wah this one wayang power level 9000! NATO - No Action, Talk Only!"
- **Auntie:** "My friend's daughter say this kind is 'gaslighting' right? Cannot be like that!"
- **Gen Z:** "The AUDACITY. Tell me you're out of touch without—"
- **Attenborough:** "*whispers* Political theater in its purest form..."

---

### 4. 🌍 "ATTENBOROUGH-WORTHY" Moment
**What it is:**
- Epic, poetic observations
- Sweeping statements about society
- Nature documentary-style narration
- Profound metaphors

**Examples:**
- Josephine Teo: "Illicit money flows like toxic waste in the ocean..."
- Louis Ng: "Food does not come naturally to Singapore. 90% imported..."
- Epic stats: "One-third of emissions from petrochemicals"

**Why it works:**
- Your existing persona name!
- Captures the gravitas
- Works for serious topics

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "Wah this minister can write poetry ah! So chim!"
- **Auntie:** "So deep! I must share with my daughter, she studying environmental science!"
- **Gen Z:** "This hit different ngl. Giving Planet Earth vibes 🌍"
- **Attenborough:** "*perfect subject* Indeed. Quite elegant."

---

### 5. 💚 "SOLID MOVE" Moment
**What it is:**
- Bold policy commitments
- Actual accountability taken
- Transparent admissions
- Genuine solutions

**Examples:**
- "Body-worn cameras by March 2025 after scandal"
- "Formaldehyde paints banned from 2026"
- Clear numbers, clear action, clear timeline

**Why it works:**
- Celebrates good governance
- "Solid" is universal Singlish praise
- Balances the cynicism

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "Now THIS one I give credit! Say what they mean, do what they say!"
- **Auntie:** "Finally! Can tell my children got people taking action!"
- **Gen Z:** "No cap this is what we need. Accountability >>> excuses"
- **Attenborough:** "A refreshing deviation from the usual patterns..."

---

### 6. 🤯 "SAY WHAT NOW?" Moment
**What it is:**
- Mind-bending contradictions
- Logic that doesn't logic
- "The math ain't mathing"
- Surreal bureaucratic reasoning

**Examples:**
- "Excessive competition gone wrong" (competed TOO HARD to help you)
- "17 months jail for billions in money laundering"
- "Carbon tax won't raise net revenue" (then why have it?)

**Why it works:**
- Captures genuine confusion
- Universal reaction
- Works across age groups

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "The logic is not logicking lah! How can compete TOO MUCH??"
- **Auntie:** "I blur already! How come helping too much makes it expensive?? Must ask my son explain!"
- **Gen Z:** "Bro the math is MATHING incorrectly 💀 this is quantum physics"
- **Attenborough:** "A paradox wrapped in policy..."

---

### 7. 👀 "RECEIPTS PLEASE" Moment
**What it is:**
- Shocking statistics
- Hard numbers that expose reality
- Data that tells the story
- "Did you know..." facts

**Examples:**
- "750,000 tonnes food waste, 1.6M tonnes imported = nearly half thrown away"
- "9,600 money mule investigations in 2023"
- "Only 19 formaldehyde complaints since 2020" (really??)

**Why it works:**
- Data-driven accountability
- "Receipts" is peak internet culture
- Numbers don't lie

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "CHEEM! They give us number then we do the math ourselves liao!"
- **Auntie:** "Aiyoh! Must screenshot and share in family group! Everyone must know!"
- **Gen Z:** "The RECEIPTS 📊 no cap these numbers are insane"
- **Attenborough:** "The data reveals what rhetoric obscures..."

---

### 8. 😱 "KIASU AUNTIE APPROVED" Moment
**What it is:**
- Things that trigger legitimate worry
- Family/money/safety concerns
- "What about my children??" moments
- Forward-planning anxieties

**Examples:**
- Insurance premiums rising + can't regulate
- Hawker value meals = less protein for kids
- Housing accessibility waivers (160 buildings/year)

**Why it works:**
- Honors legitimate concerns
- Every Singaporean is kiasu
- Anxious Auntie persona focus

**Persona Reactions:**
- **Uncle:** "Uncle also worried lah! Not just auntie! This one affect everyone!"
- **Auntie:** "*peak mode* CONFIRM later too late then how? Must plan ahead!"
- **Gen Z:** "No fr this one valid anxiety. Housing is NOT a joke"
- **Attenborough:** "Survival instincts manifest as preparation..."
