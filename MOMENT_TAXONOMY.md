# Complete Moment Taxonomy
**Comprehensive classification system for parliamentary moments**

---

## 🎯 PHILOSOPHY

**Two-Tier System:**
1. **Moment TYPES** (20-30 types) - What we extract from transcripts
2. **Voting CATEGORIES** (8 categories) - Curated subset for public voting ("Most ___ Moment")

**Purpose:**
- Extract DIVERSITY of moments (cast wide net)
- Let users vote on ENGAGING categories (shareable subset)
- Power features like filtering, search, analytics

---

## 📊 TIER 1: MOMENT TYPES (Complete Extraction Taxonomy)

### BUREAUCRACY & EVASION (7 types)

#### 1. 🙈 Don't Ask Me
**Definition:** "We don't track that" / "Can't disclose"
**Examples:**
- HDB: "We don't collect info on self-employed owners"
- Edwin Tong: "We don't know the cost"
**Persona Fit:** Uncle, Auntie

#### 2. 😤 Push Ball
**Definition:** Deflecting responsibility to citizens
**Examples:**
- "Fix formaldehyde? Just ventilate"
- "Insurance expensive? Be flexible"
**Persona Fit:** Uncle, Gen Z

#### 3. 🏓 Political Ping-Pong
**Definition:** MPs batting questions back/forth, covering for each other
**Examples:**
- "I'll defer to my colleague" → "My colleague answered this" → loop
- MPs tag-teaming to avoid direct answers
**Persona Fit:** Uncle, Attenborough

#### 4. 🎭 Wayang King/Queen
**Definition:** Political theatre, performative concern
**Examples:**
- Teo Chee Hean: "You missed the reports" (dismissive after scandal)
- Praise immediately after someone got grilled
**Persona Fit:** Uncle, Gen Z

#### 5. 🌀 Circle Talk
**Definition:** Answering without answering, verbal loops
**Examples:**
- 500 words that say nothing
- "As I mentioned earlier..." (but didn't actually mention)
**Persona Fit:** Attenborough, Gen Z

#### 6. 🎯 Moving Goalposts
**Definition:** Shifting criteria/definitions to avoid accountability
**Examples:**
- Redefining what "affordable" means
- Changing success metrics mid-discussion
**Persona Fit:** Uncle, Gen Z

#### 7. 🔒 Classified
**Definition:** "Not in public interest to disclose"
**Examples:**
- Refusing to reveal costs/contracts
- Security/sensitivity excuses
**Persona Fit:** Uncle, Auntie

### ABSURDITY & LOGIC FAILS (6 types)

#### 8. 🤯 Say What Now?

**Examples:**

#### 9. 🧮 Math Ain't Mathing
**Definition:** Numbers that don't add up
**Examples:**
- "17 months jail for billions laundered"

**Persona Fit:** Gen Z, Uncle

#### 10. 📖 Doublespeak
**Definition:** Bureaucratic jargon masking simple truths
**Examples:**
- "Knot that insurers find themselves in"

**Persona Fit:** Gen Z, Attenborough

#### 11. ⚡ Oxymoron Alert
**Definition:** Inherently contradictory statements
**Examples:**
- "Mandatory voluntary participation"

#### 12. 🤔 Wait, Run That By Me Again
**Definition:** Statements that require double-take
**Examples:**
- Something so confusing you need to rewind

**Persona Fit:** All personas

#### 13. 🎪 Circus Logic
**Definition:** When the explanation is more confusing than the problem
**Examples:**
- Complex schemes that make simple things complicated
- Byzantine reasoning
**Persona Fit:** Attenborough, Uncle

- Each voting category maps to multiple types
- Can change voting categories seasonally

## 🎯 EXTRACTION STRATEGY

### Primary Extraction (Claude/Gemini prompt)
Extract ALL moment types, tag with:
```json
{
  "quote": "...",
  "moment_types": ["push_ball", "tone_deaf"],  // Can have multiple
  "primary_type": "push_ball",                 // Main classification
  "voting_category": "push_ball",              // Which of 8 voting categories
  "virality_score": 8.5
}
```

### Post-Processing
- **Deduplicate** across types (same quote tagged multiple ways)
- **Quality filter** (min virality score)
- **Balance** (don't over-represent one type)
- **Voting prep** (map to 8 categories for UI)
